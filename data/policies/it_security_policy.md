# IT Security Policy

This policy defines minimum security requirements for company-issued devices,
credentials, and access to internal systems.

## Password Requirements

All employee accounts must use passwords that meet the company's minimum
complexity standard and are rotated on a defined schedule.

### Minimum Complexity

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and include a mix of uppercase,
lowercase, numeric, and special characters. Reused passwords from the last 10
rotations are not permitted.

### Rotation Schedule

Passwords for systems classified as high-sensitivity must be rotated every 90
days. Standard-sensitivity systems require rotation every 180 days.

## Device Security

Company-issued laptops and phones must have disk encryption, a screen lock,
and endpoint monitoring software enabled at all times.

### Lost or Stolen Devices

Any lost or stolen company device must be reported to the IT security team
within 1 hour of discovery so it can be remotely wiped and access revoked.

## Access Requests

Access to internal systems beyond an employee's default role requires manager
and system-owner approval.

### Approval Workflow

Access requests are submitted through the IT service desk and require
approval from both the requesting employee's manager and the owner of the
target system before provisioning.
