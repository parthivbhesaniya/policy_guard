# Employee Leave Policy

This policy describes annual leave, sick leave, and parental leave entitlements
for all full-time employees.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of annual leave per month, for a total of
21 days per calendar year. Leave must be requested at least 5 business days in
advance through the HR portal.

### Carryover Rules

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next
calendar year. Any unused days beyond this cap are forfeited on December 31st
unless the employee has an approved carryover exception on file with HR.

### Requesting Leave

Leave requests are submitted via the HR portal and require manager approval.
Requests submitted with less than 5 business days notice may be denied except
in documented emergencies.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick leave does not
carry over between calendar years and does not require advance notice, but
absences longer than 3 consecutive days require a doctor's note.

### Doctor's Note Requirement

For sick leave absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days, employees must
submit a doctor's note to HR within 5 business days of returning to work.

## Parental Leave

Eligible employees receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth
or adoption of a child.

### Eligibility

Employees must have completed at least 6 months of continuous employment to
be eligible for paid parental leave. Employees below this threshold are
eligible for unpaid leave under applicable local law.
